📜 Game Rules

  • Turn & Dice: Teams take turns clicking the dice button to see how many opponent balloons they can pop.
  • English Question: After rolling, a question appears. The team must answer it correctly before the timer runs out (if enabled).
  • Teacher Verification: The teacher clicks Reveal answer and then Correct (the team rolls the dice again to pop opponent balloons) or Incorrect (ends the turn immediately).
  • Winning: The first team to pop all opponent balloons wins the game!
